Twiddo Support

Twiddo is a two-thumb brain game for iPhone, iPhone Duo and iPad.

How to play

Hold your device sideways (or open iPhone Duo like a book) and keep one thumb on each critter. Each thumb follows its own move, and the moves switch every few seconds. Keep both in sync to score.

Common questions

My progress is gone. Progress is saved on your device. If you enabled iCloud sync in Twiddo's Data settings, check that iCloud is available and that sync is still enabled. Deleting the app removes its local save; with iCloud sync enabled, the save may also be available through your iCloud account.

Leaderboards do not show. Game Center is optional. To use leaderboards and friends' Daily Sync scores, sign in to Game Center in the Settings app, then reopen Twiddo.

Two players? Choose Co-op or Versus from the Modes screen; each player takes one side of the screen.
