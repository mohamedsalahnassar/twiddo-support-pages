Twiddo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Twiddo is a game made by Mohamed Nassar. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

Information Twiddo collects

Twiddo does not operate its own accounts, advertising, analytics or third-party tracking, and the developer does not receive your personal information or game data.

Game data and iCloud

Your progress, scores, unlocked items and settings are saved on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync in Twiddo's Data settings, Twiddo stores your game save in your personal iCloud account so it can sync across your devices. You can turn sync off in the same settings. iCloud is provided by Apple and is subject to Apple's privacy policy.

Game Center

If you use Apple Game Center, Twiddo uses your Game Center account to submit eligible scores and achievements and, where applicable, show friends' Daily Sync scores and display names. This information is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Game Center is optional; local play and scores work without it.

Children

Twiddo is designed for all ages. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Apple may process information through iCloud or Game Center when you choose to use those services.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
